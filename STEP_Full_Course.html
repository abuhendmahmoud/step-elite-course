03

قاعدة ذهبية — ما فيش خصم على الإجابة الغلط!

No Penalty for Wrong Answers — Always Guess

خلي بالك من دي كويس: اختبار STEP ما بيخصمش على الإجابة الغلط. يعني لو سبت سؤال فاضي = 0 بالتأكيد. لو خمّنت عشوائياً = 25% فرصة للصح. لو حذفت خيارين غلط أولاً = 50% فرصة.

🚫 القاعدة: ماتتركش سؤال فاضي أبداً — حتى لو ما تعرفش، خمّن.

04

Skimming و Scanning — إيه الفرق؟

Two Reading Strategies, Two Different Goals

⚡ Skimming — الفهم العام
اقرأ بسرعة عشان تاخد الفكرة العامة. ما بتقرأش كل كلمة. ركّز على أول جملة في كل فقرة (topic sentence) والكلمات البولد.

🔍 Scanning — البحث عن معلومة
ابحث عن كلمة أو رقم أو اسم محدد. الأسئلة بتيجي بترتيب الفقرات — سؤال 1 = فقرة 1، سؤال 2 = فقرة 2. استخدم ده لصالحك.

الشرح · الطريقة الصح للقراءة

الخطوات الخمس — الطريقة الصح لقراءة نص STEP

The Proven 5-Step Approach for STEP Reading
  1. اقرأ النص مرة سريعة (30 ثانية) — Skim. فكرة عامة بس، ما تغوصش في التفاصيل.
  2. اقرأ السؤال الأول ثم Scan الفقرة الأولى للإجابة.
  3. حدّد الإجابة وانتقل فوراً للسؤال الجاي + فقرة جديدة.
  4. السؤال الصعب؟ سيبه وارجعله بعدين. خلّص السهل الأول.
  5. سؤال الفكرة الرئيسية → أجب عليه آخر حاجة دايماً. لأن بعد ما تخلّص الأسئلة تانية بتبقى فاهم النص أكتر.
الشرح · استراتيجية الحذف

POE — Process of Elimination

When Unsure — Eliminate First, Then Guess

✂️ احذف أي إجابة تتعارض مع النص مباشرة.

✂️ احذف أي إجابة فيها كلمات متطرفة: always / never / all / none — غالباً غلط.

✂️ احذف أي إجابة صح في العموم بس مش مذكورة في النص.

✂️ احذف أي إجابة صح جزئياً فقط بس مش كلها صح.

🪮 أكبر فخ في STEP: إجابة صح في نفسها بس ما بتجاوبش على السؤال المطروح. دايماً اسأل نفسك: «هل ده بيجاوب على دا السؤال تحديداً؟»

✔ الإجابة B — 58

سؤال 2 من 3
You have 2 minutes left and 4 unanswered questions. The BEST strategy is:
✔ الإجابة B

STEP ما بيخصمش على الإجابة الغلط. ما تسيبش سؤال فاضي — خمّن دايماً. الحذف بيرفع احتمال الصواب من 25% لـ 50%.

Which of these is a "distractor" technique used in STEP multiple-choice questions?
✔ الإجابة C

🔁 A — Word Order (Most Repeated Grammar Topic)

تسريبات ▼

Key Rules

Adverb of Frequency Placement — Always/usually/often/never go BEFORE the main verb but AFTER "be": She always arrives early. He is never late.
Adjective Order — Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Colour → Origin → Material → Noun: a beautiful small old round red Italian wooden table
Indirect Questions — Become statement word order after "know/wonder/ask": I don't know where he is. (NOT: where is he)

⚡ Drill A — Word Order 🔁 High Recurrence

07:00
1Choose the sentence with CORRECT word order.
A She goes always to the gym before work.
B She always goes to the gym before work.
C Always she goes to the gym before work.
D She goes to the always gym before work.
✔ B Frequency adverbs go BEFORE the main verb (goes).
ظروف التكرار تأتي قبل الفعل الرئيسي مباشرةً (goes).
2Can you tell me _______ ?
A where is the nearest pharmacy
B where the nearest pharmacy is
C where is nearest pharmacy the
D the nearest pharmacy where is
✔ B Indirect questions use statement word order: subject + verb.
الأسئلة غير المباشرة تستخدم ترتيب الجملة العادي: فاعل + فعل (بدون عكس).
3He _______ speaks English well.
A rarely
B yesterday
C since
D already
✔ A Rarely is a frequency adverb that fits before the verb. Yesterday/since don't fit. Already usually goes in perfect tenses.
Rarely = ظرف تكرار يناسب ما قبل الفعل. Yesterday/since لا يناسبان. Already عادةً مع الأزمنة التامة.
4I wonder _______ the meeting will be held.
A that where
B where will
C where
D when will it
✔ C "I wonder where" begins an indirect question. No auxiliary inversion needed — the rest of the sentence provides subject + verb.
'I wonder where' = سؤال غير مباشر. لا يلزم عكس الفاعل والفعل — بقية الجملة تُكمل المعنى.
5Which is correct?
A It is a small beautiful Italian car.
B It is a beautiful small Italian car.
C It is an Italian beautiful small car.
D It is a small Italian beautiful car.
✔ B Adjective order: Opinion (beautiful) → Size (small) → Origin (Italian).
ترتيب الصفات: رأي (beautiful) → حجم (small) → أصل (Italian).
6Nobody knows _______ he resigned.
A why did
B that why
C why
D why he did
✔ C "Nobody knows why he resigned" — indirect question, no inversion of auxiliary.
'Nobody knows why he resigned' — سؤال غير مباشر، لا عكس للمساعد.

🔁 B — If-Clauses / Conditionals (Repeated Every Year)

تسريبات ▼

Quick Reference

Type 0 (Always True) — If + present simple, present simple: If you heat ice, it melts.
Type 1 (Real Future) — If + present simple, will + base: If it rains, we will stay home.
Type 2 (Unreal Present) — If + past simple, would + base: If I had a car, I would drive to work.
Type 3 (Impossible Past) — If + past perfect, would have + past participle: If she had studied, she would have passed.

⚡ Drill B — Conditionals 🔁 High Recurrence

08:00
1If she _______ earlier, she would not have missed the bus.
A leaves
B left
C had left
D would leave
✔ C Type 3 conditional — if + past perfect (had left) → would have + past participle (missed).
Third Conditional — موقف مستحيل في الماضي: if + past perfect (had left) → would have + pp (missed).
2If I _______ you, I would apologize immediately.
A am
B were
C have been
D would be
✔ B Type 2 conditional — "If I were you" is a fixed expression. Use were for all subjects in Type 2.
Type 2 Conditional — 'If I were you' = تعبير ثابت. استخدم 'were' مع جميع الضمائر في Type 2.
3Water boils if you _______ it to 100°C.
A will heat
B would heat
C heat
D had heated
✔ C Type 0 — scientific facts use present simple in both clauses.
Type 0 — حقائق علمية: present simple في الجملتين. إذا سخّنت الماء يغلي — دائماً صحيح.
4If they _______ the project on time, the client would be very pleased.
A finish
B finished
C had finished
D will finish
✔ B Type 2 — unreal present: if + past simple → would + base.
Type 2 — غير واقعي في الحاضر: if + past simple → would + base. أتمنى ذلك لكنه غير حقيقي.
5If you press this button, the alarm _______ .
A would ring
B rang
C will ring
D had rung
✔ C Type 1 — real/likely future: if + present → will + base.
Type 1 — مستقبل محتمل: if + present → will + base. شرط حقيقي وممكن.
6She would have passed the exam if she _______ harder.
A studies
B studied
C had studied
D would study
✔ C Type 3 — impossible past: would have + pp → if + past perfect.
Type 3 — مستحيل في الماضي: would have + pp → if + past perfect. لا يمكن تغييره.
7Unless you _______ your ticket, you cannot board the plane.
A show
B will show
C showed
D would show
✔ A Unless = if … not. It functions like Type 1 and takes present simple.
Unless = if … not. يعمل كـ Type 1 ويأخذ Present Simple.

🔁 C — Gerund vs Infinitive (Appears in Every Exam)

تسريبات ▼

The Golden Lists

Always Gerund (-ing) — enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, suggest, consider, keep, practise, deny, admit: "He avoided making eye contact."
Always Infinitive (to + base) — want, decide, agree, plan, hope, expect, need, manage, afford, refuse: "She decided to leave early."
Both — Different Meaning — stop/remember/forget/try: "He stopped to smoke" (paused in order to) vs "He stopped smoking" (quit).

⚡ Drill C — Gerund vs Infinitive 🔁 High Recurrence

07:00
1The doctor suggested _______ more water every day.
A to drink
B drinking
C to drinking
D drink
✔ B Suggest always takes gerund: suggest + -ing.
Suggest يأخذ دائماً الجرند: suggest + -ing.
2She agreed _______ the contract the next day.
A signing
B to sign
C signed
D on signing
✔ B Agree takes infinitive: agree + to + base form.
Agree يأخذ المصدر: agree + to + فعل مجرد.
3I remember _______ that book when I was a child. It was wonderful.
A reading
B to read
C to have read
D read
✔ A Remember + -ing = remember a past action. Remember + to = don't forget to do something future.
Remember + -ing = تذكّر فعلاً حدث في الماضي. Remember + to = لا تنسَ أن تفعل شيئاً في المستقبل.
4They kept _______ even though the teacher asked them to stop.
A to talk
B talking
C talk
D to talking
✔ B Keep always takes gerund: keep + -ing = continue doing.
Keep يأخذ دائماً الجرند: keep + -ing = الاستمرار في الفعل.
5He stopped _______ because he was late, and drove off quickly.
A waving
B to wave
C wave
D having waved
✔ A "Stopped waving" = quit the action of waving. "Stopped to wave" would mean he paused in order to wave — but the context says he drove off, so he stopped (quit) waving.
'Stopped waving' = توقف عن التلويح (أقلع). 'Stopped to wave' = توقف من أجل التلويح — لكن السياق يقول انطلق بسيارته، فإذن توقف عن التلويح.
6The company refused _______ the workers' demands.
A meeting
B met
C to meet
D meeting with
✔ C Refuse takes infinitive: refuse + to + base.
Refuse يأخذ المصدر: refuse + to + فعل مجرد.

🔁 D — Subject-Verb Agreement: Tricky Cases

تسريبات ▼

The Traps

Collective Nouns — team/committee/government + SINGULAR verb in American English: The team is ready.
Indefinite Pronouns — everyone/someone/nobody/each/either/neither → SINGULAR: Everyone is welcome.
Neither…nor / Either…or — verb agrees with NEAREST subject: Neither the students nor the teacher is here.
Prepositional Phrase Distractor — Ignore "of the…": A box of chocolates is on the table.

⚡ Drill D — SVA 🔁 High Recurrence

06:00
1Each of the students _______ expected to submit the assignment by Friday.
A are
B is
C were
D have been
✔ B Each is always singular. Ignore "of the students".
Each = مفرد دائماً. تجاهل 'of the students'.
2Neither the manager nor the employees _______ informed about the change.
A was
B were
C is
D has been
✔ B Neither…nor: verb agrees with nearest subject (employees = plural → were).
Neither…nor: الفعل يتطابق مع الاسم الأقرب (employees = جمع → were).
3The news _______ very shocking to everyone in the office.
A was
B were
C have been
D are
✔ A News looks plural but is an uncountable singular noun → singular verb.
News تبدو جمعاً لكنها اسم غير معدود مفرد → فعل مفرد.
4A number of scientists _______ working on a cure for the disease.
A are
B is
C was
D has been
✔ A "A number of + plural noun" takes a PLURAL verb. (Contrast: "The number of scientists IS growing." )
'A number of + اسم جمع' → فعل جمع. قارن: 'The number of scientists IS growing.' (مفرد).
5Everyone in the meeting rooms _______ told to switch off their phones.
A was
B were
C have been
D are
✔ A Everyone = singular always. Ignore "in the meeting rooms".
Everyone = مفرد دائماً. تجاهل 'in the meeting rooms'.

🔁 E — Connectors & Signal Words

تسريبات ▼

The Four Groups

Contrast — however, although, even though, despite, in spite of, whereas, on the other hand, nevertheless
Cause/Effect — because, since, as a result, therefore, consequently, thus, due to, owing to
Addition — moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides, also, not only…but also
Condition — if, unless, provided that, as long as, on condition that
💡 STEP Trap: Despite / in spite of are followed by a NOUN or -ing phrase, NOT a clause. "Despite being tired" ✔ — "Despite he was tired" ✗

⚡ Drill E — Connectors 🔁 High Recurrence

07:00
1_______ his lack of experience, he was offered the position.
A Although
B Despite
C However
D Even though
✔ B "His lack of experience" is a noun phrase → Despite (preposition). Although/Even though need a full clause.
'His lack of experience' = عبارة اسمية → استخدم حرف جر Despite. Although/Even though تحتاج جملة كاملة.
2The project was delayed; _______, the team managed to deliver it by year-end.
A therefore
B nevertheless
C furthermore
D similarly
✔ B Nevertheless = despite that. Contrast connector joining two contrasting ideas.
Nevertheless = بالرغم من ذلك. رابط تعارض يربط فكرتين متضادتين.
3The company invested heavily in training; _______, productivity improved significantly.
A however
B as a result
C whereas
D in contrast
✔ B Cause → effect relationship. As a result introduces the outcome.
علاقة سبب → نتيجة. 'As a result' يُقدّم النتيجة.
4_______ it was raining heavily, the match was not cancelled.
A Despite
B Although
C In spite of
D Because of
✔ B "It was raining" is a full clause → need subordinating conjunction Although (or Even though). Despite/In spite of need noun phrases.
'It was raining' = جملة كاملة → استخدم أداة ربط Although (أو Even though). Despite/In spite of تحتاج عبارة اسمية.
5The medicine is effective; _______, it has several side effects.
A therefore
B however
C moreover
D similarly
✔ B Contrast between "effective" and "side effects" → however.
تعارض بين 'effective' و'side effects' → however (ومع ذلك).
6The government launched new policies. _______, it increased funding for public services.
A However
B Furthermore
C Although
D Consequently
✔ B Both sentences show government actions = addition → Furthermore.
كلا الجملتين تُظهران أفعال حكومية = إضافة → Furthermore (فضلاً عن ذلك).

🔁 F — Vocabulary in Context (Most Repeated Words)

تسريبات ▼

High-Frequency STEP Vocabulary

allocate (v) — to assign resources/money/time for a purpose: The ministry allocated funds to education.
significant (adj) — large and important: The research produced significant findings.
derive (v) — to obtain or originate from: Many English words derive from Latin.
conduct (v/n) — to carry out (research/survey); behaviour: The team conducted a survey. His conduct was professional.
distinct (adj) — clearly different or separate: The two regions have distinct cultures.
implement (v) — to put a plan into action: The school implemented a new curriculum.
adequate (adj) — enough, sufficient: Students need adequate sleep before exams.

4The new law was _______ across all regions starting January 1st.
A conducted

1The manager is responsible _______ training all new staff members.

تسريبات ▼

Passive Formula & Tense Sequence

Passive Voice = be + past participle (+ by + agent optional): The report was written by the director.
Tense in Passive — Present simple: is/are + pp | Past simple: was/were + pp | Perfect: has/have been + pp | Future: will be + pp | Continuous: is being + pp
Sequence of Tenses — After past reporting verbs (said, told, thought), shift the tense: She said she "was leaving" (not "is leaving").
