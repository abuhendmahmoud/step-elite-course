1 Simple Sentence الجملة البسيطة

One subject + one predicate. It can stand alone.
فاعل واحد + مُسند واحد = جملة كاملة لوحدها (تُسمّى Independent Clause)

2 Compound Sentence الجملة المركّبة

Two simple sentences joined with a comma + conjunction.
جملتين بسيطتين، نربطهم بـ فاصلة + أداة ربط (and / but / or / so)

3 Complex Sentence الجملة المعقّدة

One independent clause + one dependent clause.
جملة مستقلة (تقف لوحدها) + جملة تابعة (ما تقدرش تقف لوحدها)
The dependent clause starts with a subordinating conjunction.
الجملة التابعة بتبدأ بأداة زي: because, when, if, before, after, although
🟪 When she was better , 🟩 she went outside.
When she was better
DEPENDENTتابعة 🙋 محتاجة مساعدة
,
COMMA
she went outside.
INDEPENDENTمستقلة 💪
🤔 Say only “When she was better.” → Incomplete! What happened? 😕
لو قلت "لما بقت أحسن." بس → الكلام ناقص! حصل إيه؟ عشان كده اسمها "تابعة".

✂️ The Comma Rule قاعدة الفاصلة

When she was better, she went outside.
Dependent clause first → use a comma ✅
التابعة في الأول → نحط فاصلة

🔑 Subordinating Conjunctions أدوات الجملة التابعة

afterبعد
After the girl took the medicine, she rested.
althoughبالرغم من
The ornithologists arrived although the girl was sick.
beforeقبل
Before they left, the ornithologists said goodbye.
becauseلأن
They were happy because the wild turkeys had hatched.
ifإذا
If you like to study animals, you should visit a farm.
whenعندما
When she was better, she went outside.
